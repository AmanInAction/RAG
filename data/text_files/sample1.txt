How AI Works?
AI relies on a layered architecture of technologies to process data and simulate cognitive tasks:
Machine Learning (ML): Algorithms train on data to automatically improve performance without explicit programming.
Deep Learning: A subset of ML that uses multi-layered artificial neural networks to mimic the human brain.
Natural Language Processing (NLP): The technology allowing machines to read, understand, and generate human language.
Computer Vision: Systems analyze visual inputs from cameras or sensors to recognize objects and context.

Core Classifications of AI:
AI is primarily categorized by its capabilities and developmental stages:
Narrow AI (Weak AI): Systems built to excel at a singular, specific task. This is the only type of AI that currently exists.
General AI (AGI): A theoretical form of AI matching human-level adaptability and intellect across multiple domains.
Super AI (ASI): A hypothetical future intelligence that vastly surpasses all human cognitive capabilities.
